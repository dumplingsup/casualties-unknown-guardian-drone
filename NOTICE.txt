Guardian Drone 0.1.0

The drone sprites are original pixel art generated by the editable project script:
pixel-art/guardian-drone/build.py

CraftPix / Free Game Assets and Elthen's published drone previews were studied for
silhouette and part separation. Their sprites, textures and promotional images are
not embedded or redistributed. Reference links are recorded in the source project.

BepInEx, Harmony, CUCoreLib, Unity and Casualties: Unknown are separate dependencies.
Their runtime DLLs and the game's decompiled source are not included in this archive.
No affiliation with the game's developer is implied.
